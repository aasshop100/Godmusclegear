Post Cycle Therapy (PCT)

How to recover fast with Nolvadex and Clomid

Post Cycle Therapy (PCT) is a protocol run after a steroid cycle to help your body recover natural testosterone production. Without PCT, testosterone levels can remain suppressed for months — causing muscle loss, fatigue, low libido and depression.

When to Start PCT

The timing depends on which steroids you used:

  • Short esters (Tren A, Test P, Anavar): Start PCT 3 days after last dose
  • Long esters (Test E, Test C, Tren E): Start PCT 2 weeks after last dose
