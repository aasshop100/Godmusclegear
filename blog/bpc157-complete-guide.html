BPC-157: The Complete Guide

Dosage, reconstitution, benefits and results

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound 157) is a synthetic peptide derived from a protein found in human gastric juice. It has gained massive popularity among athletes and bodybuilders for its extraordinary ability to heal tendons, ligaments, joints and muscles at an accelerated rate.

What Does BPC-157 Do?

BPC-157 works by promoting angiogenesis (formation of new blood vessels), stimulating growth hormone receptors in tendon fibroblasts and accelerating the healing of damaged tissue. Users report healing from injuries in 30–50% less time than normal.

  • Heals tendons and ligaments rapidly
  • Reduces joint pain and inflammation
  • Heals gut and intestinal damage
  • Protects nerves and speeds nerve healing
  • Improves overall recovery time
  • Has NO known serious side effects

BPC-157 Dosage

The standard research dosage for BPC-157 is 200–500mcg per day, typically split into two injections. Most users run it for 4–6 weeks for injury healing.

Recommended Protocol:

Dose: 250mcg twice daily (morning and evening)
Cycle length: 4–6 weeks
Injection type: Subcutaneous (SubQ) near injury site
Vial size: 5mg or 10mg

How to Reconstitute BPC-157

To reconstitute a 5mg vial of BPC-157:

  1. Add 2mL of bacteriostatic water to the 5mg vial
  2. This gives you a concentration of 2500mcg per mL
  3. For a 250mcg dose, draw 0.1mL (10 IU) on an insulin syringe
  4. Store in the refrigerator — stable for 30 days

BPC-157 Results: What to Expect

Most users notice a significant reduction in pain within the first 1–2 weeks. Full healing of tendons and ligaments typically occurs within 4–6 weeks of consistent use. BPC-157 is one of the few research peptides with a near-perfect safety profile and no reported serious side effects.
